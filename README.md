# US TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES DATA ANALYSIS 📊

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employment, growth, and workforce trends of top US technology companies. The goal is to provide data-driven insights into hiring patterns, business expansion, and industry growth using Power BI.

The dashboard transforms raw data into meaningful visualizations to support business decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives
- To analyze employment trends in US technology companies
- To understand workforce distribution across companies and regions
- To identify growth patterns and high-demand roles
- To create an interactive dashboard for business insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI** - Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **SQL** - Data extraction and querying
- **Advanced Excel** - Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **DAX** - Calculated measures and KPIs
- **Power Query** - Data transformation

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Employment Growth:** Analyzed year-over-year employment growth across major tech firms
- **Company-wise Analysis:** Compared workforce strength of top US tech companies
- **Regional Distribution:** Identified tech hubs and employment concentration areas
- **Hiring Trends:** Visualized demand for specific roles and skills
- **Business Growth:** Correlated employee growth with business expansion

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- Interactive KPIs (Total Employees, Growth %, Company Count)
- Company-wise employment comparison
- Year-wise growth trend line chart
- Regional / State-wise distribution map
- Role / Department wise analysis
- Dynamic filters and slicers
